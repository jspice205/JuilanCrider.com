Moving from California to Las Vegas: your checklist

Every week I talk with people moving from California to Las Vegas. Nevada has no state income tax, but the move itself has a few details people often miss. Here’s the checklist I give my relocation clients.

Talk to your CPA about taxes and residency

Nevada has no state income tax, but when your California residency ends and what still counts as California income depends on your situation. Get advice before you move, not after.

Plan your license and plates

New residents need a Nevada driver’s license and vehicle registration soon after moving. Check the Nevada DMV for current deadlines and book appointments early.

Budget for the heat

Look at the age of the A/C, which way the backyard faces, shade and the pool. Summer power bills are part of the real cost of a home here.

Don’t pick from a map

Neighborhoods can change from one block to the next. Tour a few areas at different times of day, or let me walk homes for you over FaceTime first.

Get a local lender early

A Nevada pre-approval makes your offer stronger and shows exactly what you can comfortably afford.
