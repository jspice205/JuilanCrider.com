New construction homes in Henderson NV

Henderson continues to add new neighborhoods, especially in the south and west. Builders compete on price, incentives and features.

Compare the real price

Ask for the base price, lot premium, upgrade budget and total estimated closing costs side by side. That’s the number to compare.

Read the incentive fine print

Rate buydowns and closing cost credits often require the builder’s lender. Sometimes that’s a good deal; sometimes an outside lender wins.

Get an independent inspection

Even new homes have issues. A pre-drywall and final inspection catch problems while the builder can still fix them.
