5 questions to ask before you buy in an HOA

A lot of homes in Las Vegas and Henderson sit in an HOA, and many sit in two. The monthly fee is the number everyone asks about, but it’s rarely the one that surprises people later. Before you write an offer, get answers to these five questions.

1. What does the fee actually cover?

Landscaping, gates and guards, pools, community water, insurance on shared areas. Two HOAs with the same fee can cover very different things, so compare what you get, not just what you pay.

2. How healthy is the reserve fund?

The reserve is the HOA’s savings for big repairs like roofs, roads and pools. A thin reserve can mean a special assessment later. Ask for the current budget and the most recent reserve study.

3. Are any special assessments coming?

Ask what’s planned or being discussed: a clubhouse remodel, new gates, road work. Board meeting minutes from the last year usually tell you more than any brochure.

4. Which rules will you actually notice?

Rentals, parking, RVs and boats, pets, paint colors, solar, backyard changes. Read the CC&Rs before you fall in love with the house, especially if you plan to rent it out or add a pool.

5. Is there a master HOA and a sub-HOA?

Many Las Vegas communities have both, with two fees and two sets of rules. Budget for both, and make sure the disclosures you get cover both.

In Nevada you’ll receive the HOA resale package during escrow, and you have time to review it. I go through it with every client so nothing in it is a surprise.
