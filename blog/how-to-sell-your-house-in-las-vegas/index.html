How to sell your house in Las Vegas, step by step

1. Know your number

Start with a valuation based on recent nearby sales, plus an estimate of your net proceeds after costs.

2. Prepare the home

Fix what inspectors will flag, declutter, and stage the main rooms. Small fixes often matter more than big remodels.

3. Price to launch

The right price draws attention in the first two weeks, when interest is highest.

4. Market it like it matters

Professional photos and video, the MLS, social media, and direct outreach to buyers already looking in your area.

5. Negotiate and close

I screen offers for terms and financing, not just price, then manage inspections, appraisal and deadlines through escrow.
