Summerlin home values: what actually moves the price

Summerlin is a collection of villages, and value changes from one to the next. Two homes with the same floor plan can sell far apart depending on where they sit.

The factors that move value

Guard-gated vs. non-gated, golf course or mountain views, lot size and privacy, and distance to Downtown Summerlin and Red Rock Canyon all show up in sale prices. Single-story homes tend to draw a deeper pool of buyers.

Condition still wins

Updated kitchens and baths, newer HVAC and roofs, and clean inspections help a home sell faster and closer to asking. If you’re thinking about selling in the next year, a walkthrough now tells you which projects are worth doing and which aren’t.
