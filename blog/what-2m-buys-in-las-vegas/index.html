What $2M buys in Las Vegas vs. coastal California

When buyers from Los Angeles, Orange County, San Diego or the Bay Area start looking in Las Vegas, the first surprise usually isn’t the price. It’s what comes with the house. Here are the five things my out-of-state clients notice first at this level.

1. Guard-gated living

Many of the luxury communities here have a staffed gate, private streets and security around the clock. For a lot of families, that peace of mind is the reason they pick one neighborhood over another.

2. Room to breathe

Bigger lots, casitas for guests or parents, RV garages and space for a car collection show up often at this level. Ask about the lot and the garage as early as you ask about the kitchen.

3. Views from home

Strip lights at night, Red Rock to the west and mountain views are common in hillside areas like MacDonald Highlands and The Ridges. The same street can have very different views, so we look lot by lot.

4. Resort-style backyards

Pools, outdoor kitchens and covered patios are built for living outside most of the year. Check the sun direction and shade: it changes how much you’ll use the backyard in July.

5. Newer construction

Much of the luxury inventory here is newer, with open layouts, smart-home systems and energy-efficient design. Older custom homes can be great value too, if you know what to inspect.
