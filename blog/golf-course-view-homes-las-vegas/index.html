Luxury homes with golf course views in Las Vegas

Las Vegas has golf communities across the valley, from Summerlin and Southern Highlands to Henderson and Lake Las Vegas.

Location on the course matters

Homes on the fairway side get views, but sometimes errant balls. Lots near greens are quieter. Elevated lots can catch both the course and the mountains or Strip.

Membership vs. HOA

Living on a course doesn’t always include club access. Membership may be separate, optional or required.

Check the course

Ask about the course’s long-term plans and water use. A course closure or redesign can change the view you paid for.
