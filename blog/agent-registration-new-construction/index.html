Why you should bring your agent to the builder’s sales office

Builder sales staff work for the builder. They’re friendly and helpful, but their job is to get the best deal for their employer.

Registration rules

Most builders require your agent to register you, or come with you, on your very first visit. If you walk in alone and sign the guest card, many builders won’t let an agent represent you later.

What an agent does for you

Compare lots and incentives across builders, negotiate upgrades and closing credits, coordinate independent inspections, and keep the timeline on track.

Builders often pay the buyer’s agent in new construction sales. Ask me to confirm for your builder before you tour.
