Henderson NV homes for sale: a neighborhood guide

Henderson is consistently one of the most popular places to buy in the valley, with a wide range of communities and price points.

Established and central

Green Valley offers mature trees, larger lots in places, and easy access to shopping at The District and Green Valley Ranch.

Master-planned

Anthem, Seven Hills and Inspirada have parks, trails and community centers, with homes from first-time buyer to luxury.

Luxury and lifestyle

MacDonald Highlands, Ascaya and Lake Las Vegas offer guard-gated hillside and waterfront living.
