Lake Las Vegas living: a lake town 30 minutes from the Strip

Most people moving to Las Vegas have never heard of it, and the ones who visit usually ask the same thing: wait, this is Vegas? Lake Las Vegas is a private, man-made lake community in Henderson, tucked into the hills east of the valley. Here are the five things buyers notice first.

1. A real lake in the desert

You can kayak, paddleboard or take an electric boat out at sunset, and walk the paths along the water in the morning. For buyers coming from the coast or a lake town, it’s the closest thing to water living you’ll find in Southern Nevada.

2. Village life

MonteLago Village has Mediterranean-style streets with cafes, shops and dining right on the water. It’s the kind of place where a weeknight dinner feels like a little getaway.

3. Golf by the water

Reflection Bay, a Jack Nicklaus design, runs along the shoreline, and SouthShore has its own course. If golf is part of your week, ask which homes have course access and how memberships work.

4. Gated and quiet

Enclaves like SouthShore are guard-gated, with hillside homes looking out over the lake and the mountains. Some neighborhoods have their own HOAs on top of the master association, so compare what each one covers.

5. Close, but away

It’s about 30 minutes to the Strip and the airport, close enough for a show or a flight, far enough that it feels quiet. Drive it at the times you’d really commute before you decide.
