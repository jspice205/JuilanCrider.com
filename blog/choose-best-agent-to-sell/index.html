How to choose an agent to sell your house

Most agents will tell you they’ll get top dollar. Ask them to show you how.

Questions to ask

What did similar homes near me sell for, and how long did they take? What would you change about my home before listing? How will you market it in the first week? How do you handle multiple offers? What happens if it doesn’t sell in 30 days?

What to look for

Specific answers, a written plan, and someone who will actually answer the phone during escrow. Reviews from past sellers tell you more than a sales pitch.
