Moving to Las Vegas from California: what to know

Taxes

Nevada has no state income tax. Property taxes on owner-occupied homes are protected by an annual cap on how much the tax bill can rise. Talk to a tax professional about your situation.

Housing

You’ll often find newer homes and more space for the money than in many California markets, along with HOAs in most master-planned communities.

The first 30 days

Get a Nevada driver’s license within 30 days of becoming a resident, then register your vehicle with the DMV.
