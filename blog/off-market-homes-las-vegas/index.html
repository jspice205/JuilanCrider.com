Off-market homes in Las Vegas: how private sales work

An off-market sale happens when a seller agrees to sell without public marketing. Reasons include privacy, timing, or wanting a quick, quiet deal.

How buyers find them

Through agents with strong relationships in specific communities. I’ve closed nine off-market sales by knowing who was thinking about selling before they listed.

Is it a good deal?

Not always. Off-market doesn’t mean underpriced. You still need recent comparable sales and inspections.
