Open houses in Summerlin this weekend: make them count

Open houses are a great way to learn a neighborhood fast. A little planning makes them much more useful.

Before you go

Pick three to five homes close together. Know the price, HOA dues and days on market for each.

While you’re there

Check natural light, noise, storage and the view from the backyard. Ask how long the home has been listed and whether there are offers.

After

Write down what you liked and didn’t. Send me the addresses and I’ll pull recent sales so you know if the price is right.
