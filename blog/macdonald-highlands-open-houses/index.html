MacDonald Highlands open houses and private showings

Guard-gated communities limit public open houses. Many showings in MacDonald Highlands happen by appointment only.

How access works

Your agent schedules the showing and registers you at the gate. Some sellers ask for proof of funds or pre-approval before a private tour.

Make each visit count

Ask to see several homes in one trip, and visit at the time of day you’d use the home most. Evening views can change your mind.
