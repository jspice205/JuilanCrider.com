The best neighborhoods in Las Vegas, by lifestyle

For outdoor lovers

Summerlin and the northwest put Red Rock Canyon and Mt. Charleston close by.

For families

Master-planned communities like Summerlin, Anthem, Inspirada and Mountain’s Edge offer parks, trails and nearby schools.

For golf

The Ridges, Red Rock Country Club, Southern Highlands, Rhodes Ranch and Seven Hills are built around courses.

For luxury and privacy

The Summit, The Ridges, MacDonald Highlands, Ascaya and Lake Las Vegas.

For quick Strip access

Southwest neighborhoods near the 215 make the commute easy.
