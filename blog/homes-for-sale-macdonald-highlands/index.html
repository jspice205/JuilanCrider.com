Buying in MacDonald Highlands: what to know

MacDonald Highlands sits in the hills of Henderson with views across the valley to the Strip. It’s guard-gated, with homes around DragonRidge Country Club and its championship golf course.

Things to ask before you buy

Is club membership separate from the HOA? What do the architectural guidelines allow for additions or pools? How is the lot graded, and what do the retaining walls need?

Views are value

Elevation and view corridors drive price here. A home that looks similar on paper can be worth much more with an unobstructed Strip view.
