What is my house worth in Las Vegas?

Automated estimates use public records and recent sales nearby. They can’t see your remodeled kitchen, the view from your backyard, or the busy street behind the wall. That’s why two websites can disagree by tens of thousands of dollars on the same house.

How I value a home

I start with homes that actually sold in the last few months, as close to yours as possible in size, age, lot and condition. Then I adjust for what makes yours different: upgrades, view, pool, single-story vs. two-story, and whether it backs to a golf course or a road.

Next I look at what’s for sale right now, because that’s your competition. Buyers compare your home to those listings, not to last year’s sales.

What you get

A written opinion of value with the comparable sales I used, a suggested launch price, and what it would take to push the price higher. No listing agreement required, and nothing is made public.
