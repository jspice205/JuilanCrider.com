How to pick your Summerlin village

Summerlin is one of the most popular places to live in Las Vegas, but it isn’t one neighborhood. It’s a collection of villages that were built over several decades, each with its own feel, home styles and amenities. Here’s how I help clients narrow it down.

Start with your daily drive

Map the places you’ll go most: work, school, the airport, the Strip, Downtown Summerlin. Drive them at rush hour, not on a Sunday. The right village can give you back 20 minutes a day.

Decide on home age and style

Older villages tend to have bigger lots and mature trees. Newer ones have modern layouts, newer systems and often smaller yards. Neither is better; it depends on how you live and how much you want to update.

Compare what the HOA includes

Pools, parks, gates, trails and community centers vary from village to village, and so do the fees. Many Summerlin homes pay a master association plus a village or sub-association.

Match it to your lifestyle

Golf, hiking and Red Rock access, quiet streets, or being able to walk to shops and restaurants. Pick the village that fits how you actually spend your weekends.
