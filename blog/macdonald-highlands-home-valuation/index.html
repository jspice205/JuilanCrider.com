Pricing a home in MacDonald Highlands

MacDonald Highlands is a guard-gated hillside community in Henderson built around DragonRidge Country Club. Homes range from semi-custom to fully custom estates, and few sell in any given month.

Fewer comparables, more judgment

With limited sales, pricing leans on view corridors, lot position, architecture, finish level and what similar buyers have paid across other luxury communities in the valley.

Launch matters

At this price point, the first weeks set the tone. Professional photography and video, private previews to qualified buyers, and a clear pricing story protect your number. I’m happy to walk the home and give you a private opinion of value.
