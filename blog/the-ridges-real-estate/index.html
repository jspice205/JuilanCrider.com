Buying in The Ridges: what to know first

The Ridges is one of Summerlin’s best-known luxury addresses, set against Red Rock Canyon and around Bear’s Best golf course.

Homes and lots

You’ll find semi-custom homes, full custom estates and vacant lots. Building a custom home means architectural review, longer timelines and careful budgeting.

Questions to ask

What are the HOA and club costs? What do the design guidelines allow? Are views protected? What does the inspection history show?

Work with someone who knows the enclave

Each neighborhood inside The Ridges has its own character and price range. I’ll help you compare them before you commit.
