New construction in Summerlin: builder vs. resale

The case for new

Builder warranties, energy-efficient systems, current floor plans, and the chance to choose finishes.

The case for resale

Mature trees, finished neighborhoods, window coverings and landscaping already in, and often more room to negotiate on price.

What buyers miss with builders

Base prices rarely include everything in the model home. Lot premiums, upgrades and closing costs add up. Incentives are often tied to the builder’s preferred lender.
