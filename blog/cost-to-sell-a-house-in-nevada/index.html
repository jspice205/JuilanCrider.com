How much does it cost to sell a house in Nevada?

Every sale is different, but the main costs fall into a few buckets.

Commissions

Agent compensation is negotiable and agreed in writing. Whether you offer compensation to a buyer’s agent is also your decision.

Transfer tax

Clark County’s real property transfer tax is $2.55 for every $500 of value, or about 0.51% of the price. In Las Vegas it’s customarily paid by the seller.

Title and escrow

Sellers usually pay for the buyer’s owner’s title policy and split the escrow fee with the buyer.

Everything else

Mortgage payoff, HOA resale package fees, any repairs or credits you agree to, and prorated property taxes. Use the seller net sheet in the app for a personalized estimate.
