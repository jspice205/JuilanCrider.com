Setting up utilities for a new home in Las Vegas or Henderson

Nothing kills move-in day like a dark house and no hot water. In Las Vegas and Henderson, the providers depend on your address, and some need a week or more of notice. Here’s how to get it all lined up.

Electric: NV Energy

NV Energy is the electric company for Southern Nevada. You can start service online through its Move Center, and NV Energy asks for at least a week of notice. Have your Social Security number, the new address and your start date ready. Service is turned on weekdays only, not on holidays, and new customers may be asked for a deposit.

Gas: Southwest Gas (if the home has it)

Not every home here has gas, so check before you call. If yours does, Southwest Gas lets you schedule service online, in its app or by phone, and it recommends booking about two weeks ahead because it uses appointment windows.

Water: it depends on the city

In the City of Las Vegas and unincorporated Clark County, water usually comes from the Las Vegas Valley Water District. You can start service online through its My Account page; there’s a turn-on fee and a deposit may be required. In Henderson, water and sewer come from the City of Henderson, and you set it up through the city’s Customer Care Center, which collects a deposit when the account opens.

Trash and HOA extras

Before you set up trash pickup on your own, check your HOA documents. In some communities, trash is already included in the dues. Your title company or the sellers can also tell you who services the street.

The takeaway

Two weeks before closing: call Southwest Gas (if you have gas) and book NV Energy and water online. Keep the confirmation numbers in one note on your phone. Your first utility bill also counts as proof of address for your Nevada driver’s license.
