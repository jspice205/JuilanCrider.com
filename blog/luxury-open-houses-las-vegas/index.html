Luxury open houses in Las Vegas: etiquette and strategy

Etiquette

Sign in, follow shoe-cover requests, and ask before taking photos or video. Sellers and listing agents remember respectful buyers.

Strategy

Treat the open house as research. Note what the home does well and what it lacks compared to others in the same price range.

The follow-up

If the home is a contender, ask your agent to request disclosures, inspection reports and HOA documents before writing an offer. In luxury, preparation is leverage.
