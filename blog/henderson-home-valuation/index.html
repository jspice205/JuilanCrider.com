Henderson home valuation: why online estimates miss

Henderson covers established neighborhoods like Green Valley, master-planned communities like Anthem and Inspirada, and hillside luxury enclaves. An estimate that works for a tract home can miss badly on a view lot.

Where estimates go wrong

Views, elevation and custom finishes rarely show up in public data. Neither do HOA amenities, which can add real value in communities with clubhouses, pools and guard gates.

The better approach
