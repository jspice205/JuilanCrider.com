Preparing a Summerlin home for market: what’s worth fixing

Usually worth it

Fresh neutral paint, landscaping cleanup, updated light fixtures, repaired fences and gates, serviced HVAC and pool equipment, and a deep clean.

Sometimes worth it

New flooring in worn areas, kitchen hardware and lighting, and replacing dated mirrors and fixtures in bathrooms.

Usually not worth it

Full kitchen remodels right before selling. Buyers often want to choose their own finishes, and the timeline can cost you a season.

A pre-listing walkthrough tells you which list your home falls into.
