Choosing a real estate agent in Summerlin

Rankings and awards are one data point. The better question is whether an agent knows your part of Summerlin and will be there when it matters.

Look for

Recent sales in your village, honest pricing advice, a clear marketing or search plan, and reviews that mention responsiveness and follow-through.

Ask for references

Talk to a past client who bought or sold something similar to what you want.

My approach

Honest about value, reliable about what happens next, and doing what I said I would do. Most of my business comes from referrals for that reason.
