Summerlin homes for sale: a buyer’s guide to the villages

Summerlin sits on the west side of the valley against Red Rock Canyon. It’s organized into villages, each with its own parks, trails, schools and HOA.

Start with lifestyle

Do you want walkability to Downtown Summerlin, a guard gate, golf, newer construction, or a larger lot? Each answer points to a different part of the community.

Know the costs

Summerlin homes often carry a master association fee plus a village or sub-association fee. Some communities also have special improvement district payments. I’ll show you the full monthly cost before you fall in love with a house.
