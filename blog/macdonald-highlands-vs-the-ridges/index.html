MacDonald Highlands vs. The Ridges: two hillside enclaves, two ways to live

When luxury buyers start looking at Las Vegas, two names come up fast: MacDonald Highlands in Henderson and The Ridges in Summerlin. They sit on opposite sides of the valley, and the right one for you comes down to your daily life more than the houses themselves.

1. Two guard-gated hillsides

Both communities are guard-gated and known for custom and semi-custom homes on hillside lots. Expect privacy, quiet streets and homes built around the view.

2. MacDonald Highlands

On the Henderson side, hillside lots look out over the valley and the Strip. The community is home to DragonRidge Country Club, a private golf club, so if golf and a club social life are part of your plan, ask how memberships work.

3. The Ridges

On the west side of Summerlin, The Ridges puts custom estates minutes from Red Rock Canyon and Downtown Summerlin. If hiking, cycling and an easy dinner out matter to you, this side of town fits.

4. Pick by your daily life

The Henderson side is closer to the airport and Lake Las Vegas. The Summerlin side is closer to Red Rock trails, shopping and dining. Think about where you’ll drive most days, not just where the house is prettiest.

5. Tour both, day and night

Lot slope, view lines, privacy and HOA rules change street to street, and many homes sit under a master HOA plus a sub-association. See both in person, in daylight and after dark, before you decide.
