Marketing alone doesn’t sell a home. Word of mouth does.

Julián Crider is a Las Vegas Realtor, new construction specialist and developer whose business has been built almost entirely on referral: clients who send their friends, families and colleagues because the experience was worth repeating.

His approach is straightforward. Be honest about what a house is worth, be clear about what happens next, and do what he said he would do. Buyers who work with him tend to see fewer houses and write fewer offers, because the guidance comes before the showings.

He also knows homes from the building side. As a partner in Westside Development Group, he is helping bring new affordable housing to the Historic Westside of Las Vegas. That experience shapes how he advises on new construction: which lots hold value, which builder incentives are real, and which upgrades are worth paying for.

The standard holds from the first conversation through closing, whether the transaction is a first condo, a new build or a country-club estate. Julián can be trusted to help you through it all.
