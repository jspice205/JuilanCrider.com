The Vegas Move Letter · No. 2

The five questions every out-of-state buyer asks me. Answered.

Hi {{first_name}},

When someone is moving to Las Vegas from another state, the same questions come up on almost every first call. Here are the honest answers, so you can skip the guesswork.

01

Can I buy without flying out first?

Yes. I walk homes with you live on FaceTime, room by room, and send video of the street and the drive. Most of my out-of-state clients see their short list in person once, and you can sign everything online.

02

How long does it take?

Once you’re in contract, escrow often runs about 30 to 45 days. The part that takes longest is usually the search, so getting pre-approved before you start saves weeks.

03

Do I need a Nevada lender?

You don’t have to use one, but a local lender who knows Nevada closings and HOA paperwork can make your offer stronger. I’m happy to introduce you to a couple I trust.

04

What does it really cost beyond the price?

HOA dues (often two of them), property taxes, the summer power bill and closing costs. Ask for real estimates on each home, not averages. I send these with every home I recommend.

05

Which neighborhood fits me?

It depends on your week more than the map: where you work, how often you fly, golf or trails, quiet streets or walkable shopping. That’s exactly what a game plan call sorts out.
